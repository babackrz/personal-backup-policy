Privacy policy

This utility is for its owner's personal use. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Drive access

The utility uses the drive.file permission to create and manage its own backup files in the owner's Google Drive. It does not request access to unrelated Drive documents.

Backup data

Selected application data, configuration, credentials and recovery files are encrypted on the source machine before upload. Google Drive receives encrypted backup files. The decryption identity stays on the owner's device. OAuth credentials are stored privately on the machine performing the uploads.

Use and retention

Backup data is used for recovery and integrity verification, not advertising, sale or model training. The owner controls the backup folder and can revoke the utility's Google access. Retention follows the owner's configuration. Older backups may be permanently deleted after a replacement backup has passed integrity verification.

This website

This static information page contains no backup files, credentials, application endpoints, analytics scripts or cookies. Its hosting provider may retain ordinary connection logs under its own policies.
